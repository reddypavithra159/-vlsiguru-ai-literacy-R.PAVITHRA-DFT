
# Week 01 - AI Engineering Journal

## Date

30-09-2026

## What I learned that was genuinely new

I learned that AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and AI Agents are related but are not the same thing. I also learned that not every automated system is AI. An important lesson was that an AI answer can sound confident and still be incorrect or unsupported.

I learned that AI assistants are useful for explanations and brainstorming, but important information should be verified using reliable sources.

## One AI output I initially trusted

I initially trusted AI-generated explanations because they were clear and confident. During the Week 1 activities, I understood that a fluent answer does not automatically mean that the information is correct.

## How I verified it

I compared answers from different AI assistants and checked important claims against reliable external sources. I also learned that calculations, code, and other results can be tested independently when possible.

## What the AI did well

AI was useful for explaining difficult concepts in simple language, giving examples, organizing information, and helping me understand new AI concepts quickly.

## What the AI could not be trusted to decide

I should not allow AI to make important decisions without human verification. For consequential work, the result should be checked using appropriate evidence and, when necessary, an authoritative source or qualified human.

## One question I still have

I still want to understand more about how AI agents use tools and make decisions across multiple steps.

## What I will do differently next week

Next week, I will not simply accept an AI-generated answer. I will first understand the claim, check the evidence, verify important information, and document what I found before using the result.
